# Twinsub privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29. Publisher: Poetical Engine LLC (hello@poeticalengine.com).

Twinsub collects nothing.

- **No account, no sign-in.** Twinsub never asks who you are.
- **No servers.** Subtitles are translated by the translator built into Chrome, on your own computer. Twinsub makes no network requests of its own. The only downloads are Chrome's own language packs, fetched by Chrome itself the first time you use a language pair.
- **What it reads.** On www.youtube.com only, Twinsub reads the subtitle files the YouTube player has already downloaded for the video you are watching, so it can show a translated line under the original. Those subtitles stay in the page and are discarded when you leave it.
- **What it stores.** Your Twinsub settings (on/off, target language, line order, which lines to show, text size and position) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in Chrome browsers. Nothing else is stored.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data.** Twinsub does not know what you watch.

Twinsub is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
